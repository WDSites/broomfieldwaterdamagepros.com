The history page groups Westlake Village with other neighborhoods developed in the 1970s and 1980s. It gives no street-level detail, so we ask for your address and cross streets instead of working from a map in our heads.

Winter

A Neighborhood That Sees Real Freezes

National Weather Service records for the Denver and Boulder area give an average first freeze around October 7 and a last freeze around May 5. That is a citywide pattern, not a Westlake claim, but it covers the months when pipes in unheated spaces are at risk.

Frozen pipes crack quietly and leak when they thaw. Our frozen pipe water damage page walks through that thaw stage, and burst pipe damage covers the first hour once a line has split.

Safety steps for a winter pipe failure

Never use an open flame to thaw a pipe. If a pipe has split, close the main shut-off first, and keep clear of water near outlets or the panel.

  • Shut the main valve before you try to find the break.
  • Open the faucet downstream so remaining water can drain.
  • Photograph the pipe, the wet area and anything damaged.
  • Call a plumber for the pipe and us for extraction and drying.

Public Lines Versus Your Own

Local note

Broomfield note: the city's after-hours water line

The city's Water Utilities page lists an after-hours line, 303-438-6400, for city water problems. A pipe inside your house is yours to fix, but a leak at the street or meter is the city's.

The history page says Broomfield's early water came from Great Western Reservoir and Denver Water through Conduit 81. It does not tie either source to Westlake Village.

Insurance

Insurance Coverage for Sudden and Gradual Leaks

Insurers often treat a sudden burst differently from a long, slow leak. Coverage depends on your policy, so ask your insurer, photograph everything and keep damaged material until it is documented. ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published reference for professional water damage work, and you can ask any company whether its crews follow it.
