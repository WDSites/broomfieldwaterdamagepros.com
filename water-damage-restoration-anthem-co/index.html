The community

Water Damage Restoration in Anthem

Anthem is a master-planned community in Broomfield. The City and County of Broomfield's history page places the Anthem and Anthem Highlands subdivisions in the 2001 to 2012 building period.

A Community Associations Institute event page describes two parts. Anthem Ranch is for adults 55 and older, with 1,333 homes planned at buildout and a 32,000 square foot Aspen Lodge. Anthem Highlands is described as multi-generational, with a 32,000 square foot Parkside fitness center.

The same page credits Newland Communities as master developer and notes more than one square mile of preserved open space. It also describes Anthem through its community associations, and their rules can shape how a water loss gets handled.

Associations

Working With Your Association

  • Ask whether the association carries a master policy and what it covers.
  • Report water that came from a neighbor's unit or a shared line to the association.
  • Ask about rules for contractors, parking and work hours before a crew arrives.
  • Keep copies of notices and photos for your own insurer.

First-hour safety steps, including for older adults

Slips are a real risk on wet floors, so move carefully and keep paths dry. If anyone in the home has trouble moving, make a clear route to the door before help arrives.

  • Close the fixture valve or the main shut-off if you can reach it safely.
  • Stay out of water that has touched outlets or appliances.
  • Use towels on small clean spills, and call us for anything that spreads.
  • Photograph the damage and write down when you noticed it.
Likely sources

Where Leaks Start in Any Home

A home's age does not tell us where a leak began. We check the usual places with a moisture meter, whatever the year on the permit.

Water heater

A weeping tank or valve wets the floor. See water heater leak cleanup.

Appliance lines

Washer, dishwasher and ice maker lines leak behind and under the unit.

Supply lines

A split fitting under a sink or inside a wall can run for hours.

Roof and windows

Rain or snowmelt can enter above a ceiling. See roof leak water damage.

Drying

Drying Larger Open Rooms

Open rooms and finished lower levels change how we place equipment, because air has to reach wet surfaces to dry them. We place air movers and dehumidifiers, then recheck readings on each visit. See structural drying and dehumidification for the full method.

Insurance

Insurance: Homeowner Policy or Association Policy?

Which policy applies depends on the building type, the cause of the water and your association documents. Ask your insurer and your association manager before work starts, and keep photos and moisture readings. ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published reference for professional water damage work, and you can ask any company whether its crews follow it.
