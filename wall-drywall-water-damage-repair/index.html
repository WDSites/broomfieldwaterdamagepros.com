Cold, damp spot

How Water Gets Inside a Wall

Drywall water damage repair in Broomfield, CO is cutting out and replacing wall board that water has weakened, after the cavity behind it is dried. The plain answer to whether a wet wall can be saved is that firm board dried quickly sometimes can, and soft board never can. Water enters from supply lines in the wall, from a floor flood that wicks up, or from a roof or window leak running down the stud bay.

Homes of different ages fail in different typical places, and a plumber or inspector can tell what a specific house has. Ceilings are their own topic, so see water damage repair if the stain is overhead.

Wall Repair Process From Flood Cut to Paint

01

Meter and map

Moisture meters and thermal imaging show how high the water went and which stud bays are wet.

02

Flood cut

Board is cut a few inches above the wet line so the damaged section comes off cleanly.

03

Insulation and cavity

Wet insulation is removed and the studs and bottom plate are cleaned and exposed to air.

04

Dry before closing

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry the framing until readings match dry reference wood.

05

Patch, prime, paint

New board is hung, taped and finished, then primed and painted. Drying always comes before this step.

Safety Before You Open a Wall

Walls hide wiring and plumbing. If water has reached an outlet, a switch plate or a panel, leave it alone and call the utility or 911 for any electrical or gas risk before calling us. Do not cut into a wall with an unknown pipe or cable behind it, and do not poke through board that may carry wiring.

What Changes the Price of Wall Repair

Height of the wet line, number of wall sections and whether trim, cabinets or tile sit on the wall all change the amount of work. Insulated exterior walls take longer to open and dry than interior partitions.

Insurance and Permits for Wall Work

Check before you close it up

Sudden and accidental leaks are what many policies address. Gradual seepage and neglect often are not, and flood is usually excluded without separate coverage, so read your policy or ask your insurer how it classifies the loss. Photograph the wall before cutting.

Mistakes: Painting Over a Wet Wall

The EPA's mold guidance says not to paint or caulk moldy surfaces, and to clean up the mold and dry the surface before painting. The same rule works for water damage. Painting or sealing a wall before it dries traps moisture, and the stain, bubbling and odor tend to return.

  • Closing the wall as soon as the stain looks gone, without a meter reading.
  • Leaving wet insulation in place because the board face looks fine.
  • Pointing a heater at the wall instead of letting air and dehumidifiers dry the cavity. For the broader drying plan, see structural drying.
