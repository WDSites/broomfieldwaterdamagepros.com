How Kitchen Water Damage Starts in a Broomfield Home

Kitchen water damage in Broomfield, CO is moisture in cabinets, subfloor and the wall behind the sink or dishwasher, and the usual answer is to find the wet areas with meters and dry them before anything is torn out. Kitchens combine supply lines, drains, an ice maker line and heavy appliances, so a slow drip can run for weeks inside a cabinet base.

Under the sink

A weeping trap, supply fitting or disposal seal soaks the cabinet floor first, then the wall behind it.

Dishwasher

A failed hose or door gasket sends water under the unit and into the toe-kick and flooring.

Refrigerator line

A small ice maker fitting can leak behind the fridge where nobody looks.

Sink overflow

A clogged drain and a left-on faucet put water over the counter edge and into the cabinet faces.

Homes around Broomfield sit in a mix of ages, and a plumber or inspector can tell you what your kitchen actually has. Where a leak shows up differs by house, so measure rather than guess.

Kitchen safety before we arrive

Close the shut-off valve under the sink or, if it will not turn, the main. Unplug the dishwasher or fridge only if the cord and the floor around it are dry. Call the utility or 911 for any water near wiring, a tripped breaker that will not reset or a gas smell.

What We Do to Dry a Kitchen

01

Locate

Moisture meters and thermal imaging map the cabinets, wall and floor so we know the true edges of the wet area.

02

Open

Toe-kicks come off and small access holes are cut where needed. Cabinets stay in place if the boxes are sound.

03

Dry

Air movers push air behind and under the cabinets, and LGR dehumidifiers pull the moisture out of the room.

04

Remove

Swollen particleboard, soaked insulation and loose flooring that cannot dry are taken out and bagged.

05

Verify

Readings are checked against dry areas before anything is closed up. Our broader water mitigation page covers the method.

Kitchen Mistakes That Cost More Later

  1. Wiping the cabinet and calling it done while the wall behind stays wet.
  2. Sealing the toe-kick back on before the cavity is dry.
  3. Using a fan only, which moves water into the room air without removing it.
  4. Putting the dishwasher back before the plumber finds the cause.
