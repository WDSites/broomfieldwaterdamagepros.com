Water From an Air Conditioner: What Happens Inside a Broomfield, CO Home

AC leak water damage in Broomfield, CO is moisture from a cooling system that escapes its drain path and soaks the ceiling, wall or floor beneath it. A cooling coil pulls water out of the air, and that water is supposed to leave through a condensate line or drain pan. When the line clogs, disconnects or the pan cracks, the water goes into the house instead.

We do not have local numbers on how often this happens here, so we will not claim it is common. The cases below are the real ways it occurs, and a technician can tell which one applies to your system.

Where the Water Ends Up

Ceilings below an air handler

Drain pan overflow often shows as a brown ring or sagging drywall in a room under an upstairs or attic unit.

Closets and utility rooms

A furnace closet with a cooling coil can leak at the floor, wetting baseboards and carpet next to it.

Attic insulation

Water soaks insulation first, then drips through the ceiling when the batt cannot hold more.

Wall cavities

A disconnected line can run down inside a wall and show up low on the drywall.

Safety steps when an AC leak reaches the ceiling

Turn the cooling system off at the thermostat so it stops producing water. A sagging, wet ceiling can fall without warning, so keep people out from under it. If water is near light fixtures, outlets or the panel, call the utility or 911 for the electrical risk before calling us.

  • Put a bucket under any steady drip and move furniture away from the stain.
  • Do not poke the ceiling unless you can do it without standing below it.
  • Photograph the stain and the unit nameplate.
  • Call your HVAC technician about the line, and call us about the wet drywall and insulation.

Insurance Fit for Condensate Leaks

A drain line that overflows once can look like a sudden event, but a line that has dripped for weeks looks gradual. Insurers weigh that difference, and policies word it in different ways.

The Colorado Division of Insurance says an insurer must provide a copy of your policy within 3 business days of a request. Ask for it, keep the HVAC service notes and photos, and check your policy for notice requirements before work begins.
