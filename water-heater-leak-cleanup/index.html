Safety steps first, at the heater

A leaking heater mixes water with a gas line or a high-power circuit. Do these in order and stop if anything feels unsafe.

  • If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before anything else.
  • For an electric unit, switch off its breaker only if you can reach the panel without standing in water. Otherwise call the electric utility or 911.
  • For a gas unit, set the control to off if it is safe to reach.
  • Close the cold supply valve above the tank.
  • Call a plumber for the heater and call us for the water.

Water Heater Leaks in Broomfield, CO and Where They Start

A water heater leak in Broomfield, CO is water escaping from the tank, a relief valve, a drain valve or the supply and outlet connections. The plain answer is that the source decides the fix, and only a plumber can tell you whether the heater can be repaired or must be replaced.

Water in the home comes from Denver Water plus Colorado-Big Thompson and Windy Gap supplies, and the city's 2023 report lists hardness of 24 to 140 mg/L. That is context only, and a plumber can say whether it matters for your tank.

How We Clean Up After a Tank Leak

  1. Pump or extract the standing water. Our page on emergency water removal explains the equipment.
  2. Check the floor, base of the walls and rooms nearby with moisture meters and thermal imaging.
  3. Remove soaked pad, baseboard or soft drywall, then run air movers and LGR dehumidifiers.
  4. Clean and deodorize the area. See water damage cleanup for debris and sanitizing.
  5. Hand off the rebuild to water damage repair once the readings are right.

Cost Factors After a Heater Leak

The price follows how far the water traveled, not the size of the tank. Carpet and pad cost more to replace than tile. A leak in a finished basement usually reaches more material than one in a garage, and the drying equipment stays until the readings say the structure is dry. Scope and price are explained before work starts.

Insurance Fit for Heater Failures

Sudden failure or slow seep

A tank that suddenly fails is often treated differently from one that seeped for months, and the heater itself may be treated differently from the damage it caused. Check your policy and ask your insurer.

Keep the old tank or fitting until the adjuster has seen it, and photograph the floor before anything is removed.

Mistakes to Avoid

Do not mop and move on when carpet sits on a wet pad, since the pad holds water against the subfloor. Do not relight a gas heater after a leak until a plumber checks it. Do not paint a damp wall.
