What Commercial Water Damage Work Covers in Broomfield, CO

Commercial water damage restoration in Broomfield, CO means removing water from a non-residential building, mapping where it traveled and drying the structure in a controlled way. We limit ourselves to extraction, drying, moisture mapping, containment and documentation.

Commercial scope is settled by phone before a crew drives out, because access, square footage and who controls the space all shape the plan. The building owner or manager has to be part of that call.

What We Do on a Commercial Loss

Extraction

Pumps and extraction units remove standing water from slab floors, carpet tile and flooring in larger rooms.

Moisture mapping

Moisture meters and thermal imaging trace water across walls, ceilings and floor assemblies.

Containment

Containment barriers and negative air machines keep demolition dust and affected areas separate from occupied space.

Drying

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers are placed to a plan and checked until readings settle.

Documentation

Photos, readings and removed materials are logged so the owner, manager and insurer have the same record.

We do not repair roofs, plumbing or building systems. A roofer, plumber or the property's maintenance contractor stops the source, and we come in behind them. The shared drying method is covered in detail on our structural drying page.

Commercial Safety Comes Before Equipment

Call the utility or 911 before us for any water near electrical gear, panels or a gas smell. Tell staff to stay out of affected areas and keep walkways clear. If sewage is involved, the area is closed until it is cleaned.

Typical Steps From First Call to Dry

Step 01

Scope call

We ask what is wet, how big the area is, who controls it and whether the source is stopped.

Step 02

Make safe and extract

Standing water is pumped out. Source repair by the proper trade runs alongside.

Step 03

Map and contain

Moisture is mapped, affected areas are isolated and any removed materials are logged.

Step 04

Dry and monitor

Equipment runs and readings are checked on repeat visits until they match dry reference points.

Step 05

Report

Documentation goes to the owner or manager for insurance and the rebuild plan.

Commercial Mistakes and How to Prevent Repeat Losses

  • Waiting for a full assessment before starting extraction.
  • Letting staff move wet inventory through dry areas without a plan.
  • Skipping documentation of the floor plan and equipment placement.
  • Treating a recurring roof or supply leak as a one-time event.
  • Closing ceilings or walls before readings match dry areas.

Insurance for a Building Owner or Manager

Commercial property policies vary widely on sudden versus gradual loss and on flood. The owner or manager should read the policy, report the loss promptly under its notice terms and keep the documentation we generate. Tenants in a shared building should tell management before they file anything.

Local note

Broomfield note: permits and building history

The city Building Division says alterations, changes of use, structural repairs, drywall and fire or flood repairs require permits. Call 303-438-6370 (option 5) before a rebuild begins.

If the loss is a city water or sewer issue, the Water Utilities after-hours emergency line is 303-438-6400. A pipe inside your building is the owner's responsibility.
