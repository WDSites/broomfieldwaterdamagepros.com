Who we are

A Local Restoration Line for Broomfield, CO

Broomfield Water Damage Pros answers calls about water damage and mold in Broomfield, Colorado and the towns around it. The business address is 2095 W 6th Ave, Broomfield, CO 80020, and the phone line is open 24 hours a day.

We built the service around one idea: when water is in your house, you need a person who explains what is happening and what happens next. That means a clear conversation about scope and price before work begins, not after.

What We Handle

Our main work is water damage: emergency water removal, water mitigation, water damage cleanup, flood damage restoration and water damage repair. Mold remediation is our secondary service, because mold so often follows a leak that was not dried in time.

How a Call Works

Step 01

You call

Describe what you see and give the address. We ask about the water source, how long it has been wet and what materials are involved.

Step 02

We explain

We tell you what to shut off, what to lift and what to photograph, and we explain how the scope and price are set.

Step 03

A crew is arranged

Same-day response is the goal when available, though we do not promise an arrival time.

Step 04

Work and records

Extraction, drying and cleanup follow, with photos and moisture readings kept for your insurer.

What We Say and What We Do Not Say

Work is carried out by licensed and insured restoration professionals. We do not publish review counts, awards or years-in-business figures that we cannot back up, and we do not promise that an insurance claim will be approved.

We name the industry standards we follow in plain language. ANSI/IICRC S500 covers water damage restoration and S520 covers mold remediation. Ask any company, including us, whether its crews work to them.

Where We Work

Our home market is Broomfield ZIP codes 80020, 80021 and 80023, from Broomfield Heights to Anthem, Arista and the Interlocken business park. We also serve Westminster, Louisville, Lafayette and Erie. See the service area page for details.

How We Explain Scope and Price

Price depends on facts we can only see on site, so we explain the factors rather than guess. They are the water category, the affected area, the materials involved, the drying days and equipment, and the rebuild scope. Each service page shows how those factors apply to its work.

Water category matters most early on. Under the S500 standard, Category 1 water comes from a clean source, Category 2 carries some contamination and Category 3 is presumed contaminated, such as sewage or rising flood water. The category decides which materials can be dried and which must be removed.

Safety Comes Before Speed

If water touches electrical equipment or a gas appliance, we tell callers to contact the utility or 911 before anything else. We would rather a crew arrive an hour later than have someone stand in water near a wet panel. The same rule applies to sewage, which we treat as contaminated until proven otherwise.

Local Knowledge That Shapes the Work

Broomfield has grown in distinct stages. City history pages date Broomfield Heights to 1955 and Interlocken's first portions to 1983 and 1986, while Anthem and Arista were built between 2001 and 2012. Those eras affect the plumbing, the materials and the way water travels through a building.

The city also publishes useful safety information. Its Water Utilities page lists an after-hours emergency line, its floodplain page offers a search tool by address, and its Building Division lists drywall and flood repairs among work that requires permits.

What We Do Not Do

We do not waterproof basements or repair foundations. If water keeps entering your home, you will need a drainage or foundation contractor as well, and we will tell you so.

We do not use online forms. Everything starts with a phone call because water damage is time-sensitive and a conversation is the fastest way to sort out the right first step.

We also do not quote a number we cannot explain. If a caller asks for a price before anyone has seen the water, we say what drives it and offer an on-site look instead, because a guess in either direction does the customer no good.
