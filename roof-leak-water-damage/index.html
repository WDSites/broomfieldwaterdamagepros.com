How a Roof Leak Becomes Interior Water Damage in Broomfield, CO

A roof leak in Broomfield, CO is water that enters through the roof covering, a flashing joint or a vent and travels down into the attic, ceiling and walls. The plain answer is that the visible stain is usually only the lowest point of a much wider wet area.

Snow adds a second route. Snow that melts on a warm roof can refreeze at the eaves and push water back under shingles. NWS records show first measurable snow near October 19 and last snow near April 28, so the exposure runs most of the cold season.

Our Approach Once the Roof Is Covered

Step 01

Trace the water

Thermal imaging and moisture meters follow the path from the entry point down through insulation and ceiling.

Step 02

Relieve the ceiling

A bulging ceiling is opened in a controlled way so trapped water drains instead of collapsing.

Step 03

Remove saturated material

Wet attic insulation and soft drywall are taken out and bagged. Debris questions go to water damage cleanup.

Step 04

Dry the cavity

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry rafters and wall cavities, with readings logged. Broader drying lives under water mitigation.

Roof Leak Safety Steps

Safety first when water comes through the ceiling

Water above you can weigh more than it looks, and light fixtures can fill with it.

  • Stay out of rooms where the ceiling is bulging or lights are filling with water.
  • If water reaches a fixture, outlet or the panel, call the electric utility or 911 before calling us.
  • Catch drips with buckets and move furniture, then do not climb onto a wet or icy roof.
  • Photograph the stain and the source before cleanup.

Insurance and Roof Water

Sudden storm or slow wear

Roof claims often turn on whether the damage was sudden or gradual, and on what the policy says about the roof itself versus the interior. Read your policy, ask your insurer how it classifies your loss and keep every photo.

Colorado's Division of Insurance says flood is typically not covered under a homeowners policy, but rain through the roof is a different question. Ask which applies to you.

Mistakes After a Roof Leak

Do not paint over a ceiling stain until the material is dry, and do not leave wet insulation in place. Painting seals moisture in, and wet insulation can feed mold growth. Avoid climbing the roof yourself. Do not wait for a dry week to call, because insulation holds water against framing long after the rain stops.
